THE ONE-MINUTE MYSTERY

The untouched coffee

A woman orders coffee every morning. One day the cup is still full, yet the café knows she arrived. What detail gave her away?

Her usual Scrabble tile pouch was on the table. The point is not the riddle. It is how small repeated details become useful patterns.

WHAT JAY IS ACTUALLY TRYING TO BUILD

A private daily intelligence service for normal people.

You should not need to understand programming to understand the product. Think of it as a very organized assistant that learns your preferences and prepares the right page each morning.

The important technical truth

Most people would not need a specially retrained AI model. The useful personalization comes first from a secure profile, selected memories, connected accounts, clear rules and feedback about what was helpful. Custom training can come later when there is enough real data to justify it.

WHY JAY THOUGHT YOU WOULD UNDERSTAND THE NEED

You already know that family situations, harassment concerns, messages, dates and competing claims can become confusing. A private tool could organize what is documented, what is alleged, what is still unknown and what needs verification, without turning suspicion into fact.
