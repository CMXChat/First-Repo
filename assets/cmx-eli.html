THE FASTEST WAY TO KNOW

Three checks can collapse the uncertainty.

Stop debating every detail inside the deal. Use institutions and professionals who are outside it.

01

Independent buyer’s lawyer

Choose an unrelated Ghanaian property lawyer. Give them the entire file and instruct them to perform a fresh search, verify the chain of title, confirm the sellers, reconcile the contracts, and report only to the buyer.

02

Regulator verification

Confirm the attorney’s identity, 2026 practising licence, good standing, and chambers registration directly with the General Legal Council and Ghana Bar Association.

03

Bank and money route

Confirm the exact client-account status through Stanbic’s official channels. The bank confirmation, contracts, and buyer’s lawyer must all identify the same destination before any payment.

SAME GOAL // BETTER ODDS

Keep the ambition. Change the structure.

Two practical alternatives show how roughly the same acquisition resources could be directed toward assets that are easier to inspect, verify, phase, and measure.

Existing propertyEarlier cash flow

Inspect the building, leases, collections, occupancy, expenses, utilities, approvals, and condition before purchase.

Phased pilotGreater upside, controlled learning

Verify a smaller parcel, build one or two units, measure the real costs and demand, then decide whether to expand.

Preferred capitalDefined exposure

Invest a fixed amount into a verified project with priority repayment, collateral, inspections, and a hard contribution cap.

Illustrative market examples: 2026 listings showed completed multifamily properties near the same broad acquisition price as the claimed land package, and smaller East Legon Hills plots that could support a staged pilot. Listings are leads, not verified opportunities.
