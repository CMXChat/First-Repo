        HONEST DEMO BOUNDARY

        Public data can be live. Private-looking records stay fictional until protected connections exist.

        • Public: weather, news and market information can use sourced public services.
        • Fictional: messages, finances, health records and company records in this demo.
        • Protected: real accounts require authentication, scoped permissions, logs and revocation.
        • Approved action: consequential changes still require confirmation.
