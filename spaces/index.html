        COORDINATION AND DAILY RITUALS

        Shared calendars, adaptive alarms, and voice belong to the same approved context.

        • Shared calendars: combine approved availability, events, responsibilities, rides, and leave-by times inside a Relationship, Family, Team, or Project Space while unrelated private events remain hidden.
        • Adaptive alarm: launch the right Space and adjust the wake-up or start routine when an early meeting, commute, weather change, travel plan, or urgent responsibility changes the day.
        • Voice: read only sections safe to speak aloud, accept hands-free updates and reminders, and show a clear listening indicator with push-to-talk or wake-word controls.
        • Control: every routine remains opt-in, reviewable, pausable, and limited by person, device, room, time, and Space.

        WHAT THIS DEMO USES

        Public data can be live, but private-looking records stay fictional until secure connections are available.

        • Public: weather, news, and market information can come from sourced public services.
        • Fictional: messages, finances, health records, and company records in this demo.
        • Protected: real accounts need sign-in, limited permissions, logs, and a way to remove access.
        • Approved action: important changes still need confirmation.
