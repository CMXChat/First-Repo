Operational limits

Authorization: Use this workbench for lawful public-source research with a defined purpose. Search results are leads, not verified facts.

Persistence: Nothing is automatically written to local storage. Exported files contain full saved queries, including supplied PII, after an explicit confirmation.

Evidence: This session log records query provenance. It does not capture search-result pages, hashes, screenshots, or chain of custody. Those belong in the future case and evidence service.
