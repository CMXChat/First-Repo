The whole idea

Personal OS in one minute

The product has one simple job: help a person or group understand the current situation, protect the right boundaries, and move the right thing forward.

Most digital tools understand only one slice of your life: the calendar knows your time, email holds your messages, task managers track assignments, and an AI chat knows only the context placed into that conversation.

Personal OS creates a stable layer above those tools. It organizes authorized information into Spaces, keeps useful memory with sources and correction controls, connects current reality to goals, and produces a focused Brief with actions that remain under human control.

See the few things that deserve attention now

A morning view, team operating brief, family plan, relationship check-in, or focused project update can come from the same underlying system.

02

Keep each context separate while coordinating safely

Personal, relationship, family, business, team, and project Spaces can each have their own people, memory, goals, data, rules, and daily view.

03

Memory

Keep useful context without giving up control

Facts, self-reports, source records, preferences, decisions, and interpretations remain distinguishable, editable, exportable, and removable.

04

Goals

Connect information to a clear direction

The system can identify blockers, ask one useful question, recommend one next action, record the result, and update the next Brief.

The central difference

The AI can change while your operating context remains yours

Personal OS owns the structure around the model: Spaces, permissions, memory, sources, goals, tools, approvals, and history. A default model can power the experience, while another cloud model or future local model can use the same controlled foundation.

Complex human coordination

The system can help people coordinate without collapsing their boundaries.

The most important shared use cases involve context that belongs to more than one person and still requires careful privacy, consent, and interpretation.

Couples

Shared plans with separate private context

A Relationship Space can hold plans, rituals, decisions, promises, shared goals, and approved memories while each person retains a separate Personal Space.

  • Compare schedules before suggesting time together
  • Track an agreement after both people approve it
  • Summarize stated concerns without declaring a winner
  • Keep private information out of the shared view

Families

A household operating layer

A Family Space can organize care, school, meals, appointments, travel, events, medication reminders, chores, and household decisions with age-appropriate permissions.

  • Show each person only what they need
  • Coordinate several calendars and locations
  • Preserve family memories and important dates
  • Escalate a missed responsibility to the right adult

Teams and organizations

One shared truth with role-specific access

A Team Space can hold the project state, responsibilities, handoffs, procedures, risks, decisions, and approved financial signals. Leadership may see broader operating context while each member sees relevant work and the information needed to complete it.

Questions

Common questions about the product

Is Personal OS an AI model?

Personal OS is the product layer around AI models, managing Spaces, permissions, structured memory, sources, goals, tools, approvals, and history while the selected model handles reasoning and generation inside that controlled context.

Why are Spaces so important?

Spaces prevent private life and shared coordination from being mixed together by defining the members, memory, goals, tools, data, and visibility rules for each context.

Can different AI models use the same Personal OS?

Yes. The operating context is designed to remain stable while the user or system selects an appropriate model, so changing providers does not require rebuilding goals, memory, permissions, or Spaces.

Does the current demo use real private data?

No. The public demo uses sourced public information and clearly fictional private-looking records because real private data requires authentication, protected storage, a secure backend, and controlled connector permissions.

Can the system mediate family, relationship, or team issues?

It can organize stated information, expose conflicts, compare schedules, summarize approved positions, track agreements, and suggest practical options, but it should never diagnose people, invent motives, take sides, or replace professional judgment.

What makes the alarm, music, and voice experience different?

The routine uses the same approved context as the Brief, allowing a selected alarm to begin a user-controlled music and voice experience before moving into spoken check-ins, reminders, or updates without forcing the user through a full dashboard.
