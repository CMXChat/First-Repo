The whole idea

Spaces in one minute

A person, couple, family, business partnership, adviser relationship, or team can open one current Brief built from the information that belongs there. The Space keeps the context behind that Brief so the next day and the next conversation do not start from zero.

Calendars know time, email holds messages, project tools track work, finance apps know money, and AI chats usually understand only the context placed in front of them. Spaces joins the useful parts around the person or group who needs them, while each record keeps a clear owner, source, and permission scope.

The Brief then reduces that larger context to what changed, what matters now, what needs a decision, and what can wait. A conversation can begin from the whole Space or directly from a calendar, cash view, weather card, project board, document, or other section.

Spaces and boundaries

One structure can support very different parts of life

A Space is the continuing context for one person or group. It can hold people, memory, goals, documents, tasks, calendars, research, decisions, history, and the permissions that determine what belongs there.

Private

Personal Space

Your own goals, schedule, finances, health notes, habits, private reflections, preferences, and memory.

Shared by approval

Relationship Space

Plans, rituals, promises, shared decisions, memories, schedules, and careful coordination between two people.

Household

Family Space

Role-based

Business Partner Space

Professional boundary

Accountant and Client Space

Approved income, expenses, bills, tax preparation, assets, goals, questions, and rules shared for a defined engagement.

Coaching boundary

Trainer Space

Training, recovery, habits, progress, check-ins, coaching notes, and the next realistic session.

Least privilege

Team Space

Member-specific work, handoffs, procedures, blockers, shared project state, leadership context, and approved metrics.

Why build it

The strongest case is the layer that remembers, connects, and protects

Established products already prove demand for AI workspaces, scheduling, family coordination, finance views, and connected tools. Spaces combines those jobs around a continuing private or shared context and turns that context into a recurring Brief.

THE PRODUCT THESIS

Models and integrations can change while the user’s context becomes more useful

People, permissions, sources, goals, corrections, routines, approvals, and history can remain useful across model changes. That continuing context is where the product can become harder to replace over time.

MARKET, IP, AND GROWTH

The idea is credible enough to test, while protection depends on the implementation

Adjacent products prove demand for the individual jobs. They do not establish global uniqueness, patentability, or freedom to operate, so any protection strategy has to focus on the actual technical mechanism and documented implementation.

PROVEN IN PARTS Adjacent versions already exist

PROTECTION SEQUENCE

Protect the implementation before deeper technical disclosure

  1. 1

    Record the invention. Save dated diagrams, prototypes, inventors, technical flows, and public disclosures.

  2. 2

    Search the mechanism. Examine scoped context, provenance, private and shared derivation, revision history, ranking, and approval controls against prior art.

  3. 3

    File only when the detail supports it. If counsel finds a viable invention, use the appropriate filing strategy around the actual implementation.

  4. 4

    Layer the protection. Keep internal ranking and connector methods confidential, clear the final brand, and preserve authorship records.

COMMERCIAL SEQUENCE

Start with one shared use case before building every scenario

POSSIBLE FIRST PAID USE CASE Remote business partners and small teams

They have an identifiable buyer, recurring shared decisions, existing work data, and a clearer path to testing paid value.

  • Build: secure identity, one reliable Brief, calendar and project connections, corrections, permissions, and approved actions.
  • Prove: activation, weekly return, Brief-to-action rate, shared invitations, correction use, permission comprehension, and paid retention.
  • Expand: add household and professional client plans after the trust controls and core behavior are proven.

Important: The public demo makes filing dates worth discussing with counsel now. United States law may provide a limited grace period for some inventor disclosures, while rights elsewhere can be stricter. This section is product strategy and does not provide legal advice. Official starting points include Patent Public Search, subject matter eligibility, provisional applications, trademarks, and copyright.

Cloudflare’s Agents SDK provides durable identity, local SQL state, real-time connections, scheduled work, recoverable execution, tools, and human approval patterns. Spaces would still define the user experience, permissions, data structure, Brief composition, and interaction design.
