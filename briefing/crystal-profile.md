# Crystal Briefing Profile

Public-safe preference file for writing Crystal-specific sections of `/news`.

## Identity and privacy

- Use `Crystal` as the normal display name.
- `Criddle` may be used occasionally as an affectionate nickname when the tone fits.
- Location must remain `Waikato, New Zealand` only. Do not identify or infer a more precise location.
- Crystal usually begins her day after 8:00 PM New Zealand time, so avoid assuming a conventional morning schedule.

## Reading preference

- Keep Crystal-facing content short and easy to scan.
- Lead with the most relevant items instead of filling every possible section.
- Her strongest recurring interests are celebrity news, major crime, and pets.

## News preferences

Prioritize:

- Major crime stories and significant criminal-case developments
- Waikato crime and safety news
- Celebrity, entertainment, and internet-culture developments
- Erika Kirk updates
- Diddy news
- Rumors and viral claims when clearly labeled `UNCONFIRMED`
- Pet and animal stories
- Major world events only when genuinely consequential

Crystal is open to a broad range of celebrity and crime coverage. Confirmed reporting, allegations, rumors, and speculation must remain visibly distinct. Minimize routine politics and generic world-news filler. She has no fixed story-order preference, so rank items by relevance and freshness.

## Beauty, fashion, and personal-interest sections

Useful rotating ideas:

- Current nail trends and design references
- New or notable Chanel bag releases
- Beauty and fashion advice
- Long-hair, makeup, accessories, and jewelry inspiration
- One concise custom-jewelry business tip
- Product photography, pricing, packaging, online listings, or seller examples

Crystal creates custom jewelry by combining and repurposing jewelry components and sells pieces online. Advice should be practical and limited to one useful sentence unless a larger feature is clearly warranted.

## Content boundaries

- Avoid the term `CMX` in Crystal-facing copy unless absolutely necessary for technical accuracy.
- Do not make Ovaro or Jay's business achievements a focus of Crystal's section.
- Do not use Jay's achievements as relationship reassurance.
- Do not force couple activities, shared challenges, or music exchanges when current private context says they are unwelcome.
- Do not infer mood from appearance, music, diagnosis, or medication.
- Do not present rumors as facts.
- Treat Jay's personal activity summaries as self-reported unless independently supported by an authorized source.

## Tone

Crystal-specific writing may be direct, concise, stylish, warm, or lightly playful. It may advocate clearly for a concern, but it must not become insulting, cruel, humiliating, or inflammatory. Avoid generic positivity, patronizing language, diagnosis, forced optimism, and advice that minimizes a stated concern.

## Preference-confidence rule

Treat repeated, calm, or directly confirmed preferences as stable. Treat statements made during acute frustration as provisional unless Crystal later confirms them. Do not convert requests such as "be mean" or global claims about another person into permanent editorial rules.

## Private-context boundary

Sensitive health information, relationship allegations, names of private individuals, screenshots, direct-message details, and precise conflict history must not be stored in this public profile. Those belong in protected short-lived context or the future backend.
